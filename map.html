⚔ The Origin

Raised roughly 8,000 years ago at the end of the Long Night. Legend says it was built by Bran the Builder with the aid of giants and the children of the forest, infused with ancient magic to repel the Others.

🏰 The Night's Watch

A sworn brotherhood taking the oath: "I shall live and die at my post... I am the sword in the darkness... the shield that guards the realms of men." They wear only black, hold no lands, and father no children.

🗡 The Castles

  • Castle Black: The headquarters at the midpoint.
  • Eastwatch-by-the-Sea: The easternmost active castle.
  • Shadow Tower: The westernmost active castle.

🐉 The Great War & Beyond

The Night King breached the Wall at Eastwatch using the dragon Viserion. After Arya Stark slew the Night King, the Wall's purpose changed. Jon Snow now leads the Free Folk back into the true north, free at last.
